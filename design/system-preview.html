Colour roles

Stone is warm and quarried, never cool gray. Pine leads; sea supports; amber is the single hot note (~3% of surface).

Type scale

Fraunces for display and numerals — it carries the brand and is never flattened to a sans. Outfit for everything the caregiver reads under time pressure.

Elevation

Surfaces are layers of light over stone, not paper on a desk. Hierarchy comes from fill opacity plus a hairline — shadow is reserved for the single topmost floating layer.

Controls

Every primary control carries the Pine settle press — 120ms in, 220ms back, no overshoot. Tap anything below to feel it.

Im Dienst Niedrig

Inputs go fully opaque — glass is for display, never for anything a caregiver types counts into under time pressure.

Data density

Numerals are tabular everywhere, so a count that ticks from 9 to 10 does not shift the row beside it.
